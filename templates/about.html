About DREAMLENS AI

DREAMLENS AI is a specialized Dream Intelligence and Emotional Narrative Discovery system. Rather than providing definitive clinical diagnoses, prophecies, or generic chat responses, DreamLens empowers you to explore the symbolic, emotional, and narrative layers of your subconscious mind through guided reflection.

Our Multi-Stage Dream Intelligence Pipeline

DreamLens does not simply wrap a language model. It processes dream narratives through a structured pipeline to extract clinical-neutral psychological features:

  • Domain Classification: Filters input text to verify it describes a dream, immediately rejecting unrelated queries or prompting for context on ambiguous symbols.
  • Dream Decomposition: Isolates characters, settings, objects, transitions, and unresolved conflicts into a structured database schema.
  • Emotional Arc Mapping: Computes changes in emotion intensity from start to finish, separating events from internal feelings.
  • Symbol Context Analysis: Interprets symbols by mapping how they interact with dreamer actions and emotions, rather than looking up generic dictionary translations.
  • Psychological Lenses: Evaluates narratives from four perspectives: Emotional Psychology, Jungian Archetypes, Narrative Agency, and Personal Growth.
  • Turning Point Simulator: Simulates alternative narrative pathways at critical blockers to assist in waking-life creative problem solving.

Our Philosophy

  • Uncertainty-Aware AI: We communicate confidence scales (low, moderate, high) and suggest waking-life scenarios that would make hypotheses relevant, acknowledging the subjective nature of dreaming.
  • Safety & Boundaries: DreamLens strictly enforces safety filters: it never predicts future events, claims supernatural messages, or attempts medical diagnoses. If distressing content indicates mental health concerns, it guides users to professional support.
  • Session Thread Memory: Compares multiple dreams recorded in a single browser session to identify recurring symbols and structural themes.
